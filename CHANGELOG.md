# Changes by version

## R79

- Added built-in update checks, download progress and optional installation from GitHub.
- F9 fills maximum HP and mana; a second press restores the saved values.
- F7 is preloaded and opens above the game without a separate taskbar entry.
- Shortened the README and added Glyph unlocks, damage and enemy HP examples.

## R78 — local experiment, reverted

- Tried a different wrist and finger pose. It was not published; R79 keeps the R77 hand.

## R77

- Added a larger final step to help the last thrust reach a retreating enemy.
- Restored the transferred hand pose.
- Added bright electrical cores, a small coil inside the meter and glowing skull eyes at full charge.

## R76

- Improved thrust aiming and short follow steps.
- Full charge colours the complete eye surface.
- Hits on stunned enemies give half the normal sword charge.

## R75

- Sword trails and switching flashes now match the blade colour.
- Added a Combo controls panel to F7, side openers and directional Stasis Field.
- Fixed mixed-button jump release and flashes while the sword is hidden.

## R74

- Adjusted the trail and form-switch flash to avoid washing the sword colour out to white.

## R73

- Added a coloured trail when swinging the Reaver and a flash when changing sword forms.
- Added coloured light on Raziel and nearby surfaces. Full charge smoothly doubles the new lighting brightness.
- Lighting appearance still needs wider gameplay confirmation.

## R72

- Improved releasing target lock, including after interactions and aerial attacks.
- Added the new English F7 Field Guide layout with current controls and damage values.

## R71

- Corrected how Reaver contacts are sent to the first Kain encounter. Original vulnerability rules still apply. Full fight progress remains unverified.
- Rotated every sword symbol90 degrees anticlockwise.

## R70

- F6 now switches the base Reaver On and Off.
- Fixed opening the original Debug menu with B+X or E+left click while the mod is Off.
- Added contact support for Kain in the throne room and checked the Zephon contact rules.

## R69

- Doors, levers, valves and covers get the original game controls before attack controls.
- Fixed a check that could stop a normal ground strike from reaching the Cathedral bell.
- After an interaction finishes, the mod can return to movement and combat.
- The arena and enemy-summoning shortcuts remain removed.
- The latest mechanism and bell changes still need full gameplay confirmation.

## R68

- Added a first fix for doors blocked by attack controls.
- Removed the optional arena and enemy-summoning shortcuts.
- Test logs showed the mod returning to movement after five interactions. Lever and cover support needed more work.

## R67

- Changed Y/right click with a carried physical weapon to use the original aim, throw and finisher controls.
- Included the experimental arena from R66. It was later removed in R68.

## R66 — local test build

- Added an experimental battle arena with two enemies from each of nine non-boss groups.
- Fixed shared animation loading during enemy creation. The arena was not fully verified and was later removed.

## R65

- Updated F6 to grant the base Soul Reaver correctly.
- Updated F9 to switch the all-powers preset safely and show the result.
- Corrected the instructions for opening the original Debug menu.

## R64

- Updated the exhausted-enemy presentation after aerial attacks.
- Removed the HP/damage test popup.
- Added a standalone newcomer guide and an English-only installer.
